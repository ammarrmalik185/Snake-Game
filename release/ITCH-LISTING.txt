DESCRIPTION (paste into the itch.io description editor)

One more bite. One longer snake.

Snake is a small arcade game by ParadoX. Steer a red snake across a black playfield, collect food, and keep clear of your own growing tail.

Cross an edge to wrap around to the other side. Pick up white food for 1 point, or grab red bonus food for 5. How high can you push your score before you catch yourself?

Features
- Classic wraparound Snake gameplay
- Regular and bonus food
- Simple red, white, and black visuals
- Play directly in your desktop browser

How to play
- Click inside the game, then use the arrow keys to move.
- Collect food to grow and earn points.
- Avoid your own tail.
- Use Start to begin another attempt. Refresh the page for a fresh score.
